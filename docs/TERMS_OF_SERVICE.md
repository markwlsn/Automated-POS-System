# Terms of Service: Automated POS System (`docs/TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md`)

## 1. Acceptable Use
The Automated POS System is intended for authorized meat shop employees, customers, and shop administrators to process retail meat orders, weigh goods accurately, track inventory, and generate receipts.

## 2. Staff & Terminal Responsibilities
Staff must maintain secure credentials and ensure that weights inputted on POS terminals accurately reflect scale measurements.

## 3. Account Security
Users are responsible for safeguarding their login credentials. Any suspicious activity should immediately be reported to the shop administrator.

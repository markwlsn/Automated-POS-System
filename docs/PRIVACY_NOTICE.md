# Privacy Notice: Automated POS System (`docs/PRIVACY_NOTICE.md`)

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Customer Information**: Full name, mobile phone number, email address (for account identification and queue notifications).
- **Transaction Information**: Purchase history, item weights, order totals, payment method, payment reference numbers.
- **Staff Information**: Staff full name, email, branch assignment, transaction audit trail.

## 2. Purpose of Collection
- Order fulfillment and queue processing at counter.
- Generation of legal purchase receipts.
- Inventory tracking and sales reporting for shop owners.
- Security and audit logs.

## 3. Storage and Protection
- Data is stored securely with encrypted passwords (bcrypt) and JWT session tokens.
- No plaintext passwords or payment card details are stored.
